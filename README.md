# City Designer: Defend Humanity

**A lightweight, self-contained 3D city-building and flood-resilience game.** Build a city within a fixed budget, trigger a flood, observe damage and cascading failures, then revise your design and try again.

[Play the live demo](https://flood-lab-lele-1017.aptdune5.chatgpt.site/)

## Play

Open [`CityDesigner.html`](CityDesigner.html) in a modern desktop browser for the standalone version. It bundles the game code, styles, 3D engine, and license; no install, server, account, network assets, or CDN is required. Alternatively, host [`dist/index.html`](dist/index.html) on any static web host. WebGL 2 is recommended.

## Goal and controls

- Build within a budget of 2,000 and protect the level's target population and emergency centers. Population targets for Levels 1–5 are 64 / 90 / 108 / 133 / 152. Survival rate is shown for feedback but is not a pass/fail condition.
- Each protected resident adds one point. Best results prioritize protected residents, then fewer resident losses. Zero resident losses earn a badge.
- Place buildings: select a card at the bottom and left-click a tile. Right-drag to rotate, Shift-drag to pan, and use the wheel to zoom.
- Select buildings with keys 1–6, demolition with 7, observation with Esc, pause with Space, and undo with Ctrl/⌘+Z.
- Useful structures include regular and stilt houses, emergency centers, levees, retention basins, and pumps. Emergency centers power pumps within six tiles. Pumps draw nearby water and discharge it downstream beyond the edge of the map.
- Click **Start Flood** to run an experiment. Pause or change simulation speed, inspect water depth and event records, then restore your pre-flood blueprint and revise the layout.
- The confirmed **Clear All Buildings** action removes structures while preserving terrain; it can be undone.

## Simulation notes

The 28 × 28 terrain has multiple elevations, ridges, shallows, and low channels. Water moves between tiles according to water-level differences. Levees can be overtopped and gradually eroded; when durability reaches zero, a breach forms. Buildings take damage based on flood depth and duration. Retention basins store water up to a finite capacity; pumps have a limited rate and stop when their powered emergency center fails.

Each run has one random inflow variation between −10% and +10%. It is revealed as an in-game weather event during the flood, not before it starts. Replaying the same flood uses the same variation so layout changes can be compared fairly. A small change in inflow may cause a larger change in damage when a defense is near its limit.

This is a game-oriented, simplified water model, not an engineering-grade flood prediction tool. Individual resident movement, traffic, economy, terrain sculpting, structural debris physics, and other disaster chapters are not implemented. Future chapters and catalog items shown in the UI are previews only.

# 城市设计师：保卫人类

**一款轻量、无需安装依赖的 3D 城市建造与洪灾防御游戏。** 在固定预算内建设城市，发动洪水，观察破坏和连锁影响，再调整设计、重建并复测。

[打开线上试玩版](https://flood-lab-lele-1017.aptdune5.chatgpt.site/)

## 开始游玩

在现代桌面浏览器中打开独立版本 [`CityDesigner.html`](CityDesigner.html)。它已包含游戏代码、样式、3D 引擎和许可证，无需安装、服务器、账号、联网素材或 CDN。也可以将 [`dist/index.html`](dist/index.html) 上传到任意静态网站托管平台。建议使用支持 WebGL 2 的浏览器。

## 目标与操作

- 在 2,000 预算内建设城市，保护当前关卡的目标人数和应急中心。Level 1–5 的保护人数目标依次为 64 / 90 / 108 / 133 / 152 人。存活率会显示供参考，但不是通关或失败条件。
- 每保护一位居民获得 1 分。最佳成绩优先比较保护人数，人数相同时再比较居民损失。零伤亡可获得徽章。
- 建造：点击底部建筑卡片，再左键点击地块。右键拖动旋转，Shift+拖动平移，滚轮缩放。
- 快捷键：1–6 选择建筑，7 拆除，Esc 观察，空格暂停，Ctrl/⌘+Z 撤销。
- 可建造普通住宅、高脚住宅、应急中心、堤墙、蓄洪池和泵站。应急中心为 6 格内的泵站供电；泵站抽取附近积水，并通过暗管排到地图下游边界之外。
- 点击“发动洪水”开始实验。可以暂停或调整速度，观察水深与事件记录；灾后恢复灾前蓝图，继续调整并复测。
- 确认“清空所有建筑”后会移除所有设施、保留地形，并可撤销。

## 模拟说明

地图为 28 × 28 格，包含不同高程、山脊、浅滩和低洼通道。水根据地块水位差流动。洪水漫过堤顶后会逐渐冲刷堤墙，耐久归零时形成溃口。建筑会根据积水深度和持续时间受损。蓄洪池容量有限；泵站抽水速度有限，供电应急中心失效后会停止工作。

每轮有一个 −10% 至 +10% 的随机来水变化，会在洪水过程中以天气事件提示，不会在开始前透露。重新观看同一场洪水会保留相同偏差，方便公平比较布局改动。当防线接近极限时，来水的小幅变化也可能导致明显不同的损失。

水流模型是服务游戏玩法的简化模拟，不是工程级洪水预测工具。游戏没有实现居民个体移动、交通、经济、地形雕刻、结构碎片物理或其他灾害篇章。界面中展示的后续篇章和建筑仅为预告。
